## What's stable

Xelqun is beta. Forms, tables and panels are ready to build on, and this site's demo runs on them, but a minor release can still break your code. Every break is listed in the changelog with what to change. See [version support](/introduction/version-support) for the rules.

## What's not built yet

These are the larger gaps today. They're not promises, and there are no dates.

- **Tables:** filters above the table, drag and drop reordering, and custom summaries.
- **Import and export:** a column picker, a file of failed rows, XLSX, and queued exports.
- **Dashboards:** filters on charts, and radar, scatter and bubble charts.
- **Panels:** a QR code for two-factor setup, and tenant billing.

The [comparison with Filament](/panels/parity) goes feature by feature, and the [limits of tables](/tables/limits) has the details.
